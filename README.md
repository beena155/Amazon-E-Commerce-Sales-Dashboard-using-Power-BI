# Amazon-E-Commerce-Sales-Dashboard-using-Power-BI
An interactive Power BI dashboard designed to transform Amazon sales data into actionable insights. Track sales, profit, orders, and customer spending while exploring monthly trends, top-performing categories, regional performance, order fulfillment, and best-selling products. Built to support data-driven decision-making through dynamic visuals.

# Key Insights
Total Sales is ₹78.59M showing strong revenue performance.
Profit of ₹16M indicates healthy business profitability.
Highest sales come from the Set category and Maharashtra state.
Around 92% orders are successfully shipped, showing good fulfillment efficiency.
FBA contributes more sales than Easy Ship, making it the most effective channel.
Average Order Value is ₹652.88 showing moderate customer spending behavior.
Sales show clear monthly trends with peak performance in April.
Certain product styles drive majority of sales and demand.

# Key Features
Interactive Power BI dashboard for sales analysis.
KPI cards for Sales, Profit, Orders, Quantity, and AOV.
Monthly trend analysis for sales and profit.
Category-wise, state-wise, and style-wise performance tracking.
Order status and fulfillment method analysis.
Filters for date, category, and product size.
Easy-to-understand visual storytelling for business decisions.

# Main Motto of the Dashboard
The Amazon E-Commerce Sales Dashboard is designed to give a quick overview of business performance by showing:

How much sales revenue was generated
How many products were sold
How much profit was earned
Order volume and customer spending behavior
Best-performing products, categories, states, and fulfillment methods
Order status distribution
Its goal is to help management monitor sales performance, identify top-performing areas, and make data-driven business decisions.
